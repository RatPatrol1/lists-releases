Privacy policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Lists is a shopping-list app for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the developer of Lists does not collect, receive, store, or sell any of your data. The app has no accounts of its own, no ads, and no analytics or tracking.

Lists on your phone

Your lists are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Photos you take with the app are saved in your phone's gallery under Pictures/Lists; photos you pick from the gallery stay where they are. Backups are saved as files in your phone's Download/Lists backups folder. None of this leaves your phone unless you send it somewhere yourself.

Sending lists

When you send a list as text or as a .list file, it goes through the app you choose (for example Messages, email, or Quick Share), to the people you choose. Lists does not keep a copy anywhere else.

Shared (live) lists and your Google account

Sharing a list live is optional. If you turn it on, you sign in with your Google account and Lists asks Google for permission to:

  • Create and use files in your Google Drive (only the spreadsheets and photos Lists creates, not your other files). Shared lists are stored in a Google Sheets spreadsheet in your own Drive, and their photos in a folder next to it.
  • Read and edit Google Sheets spreadsheets, so the app can open a shared list that someone else invited you to.
  • See your name and email address, so the people you share a list with can see who joined it and who added an item.

This data goes directly between your phone and Google. It is never sent to the developer of Lists. Lists uses it only to show, change, and sync your shared lists.

Anyone who has a shared list's invite link can see and change that list and its photos. Keep the link among people you trust, and don't put personal or sensitive information on shared lists. You can stop sharing at any time, which moves your lists to a new private copy and turns off the old link.

You can remove Lists' access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The spreadsheets and photos stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Lists' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Lists does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, does not use it to train AI models, and does not let anyone read it except as needed to provide shared lists to you and the people you share them with.

Update checks

Once a day, and when you tap Check for updates, the app asks GitHub for the latest version. Like any website, GitHub sees your phone's IP address when this happens; see the GitHub privacy statement. No list data is sent.

Phone permissions

  • Camera, to take photos for your lists.
  • Photos and files, to attach gallery photos, save photos and backups, and open received lists.
  • Install apps, only to install an update you chose to download.

Children

Lists is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
