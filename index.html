Lists for Android

Lists is a simple shopping-list app for Android that makes it hard to lose items by accident.

  • Tap an item to cross it off. It stays readable.
  • Swipe an item to save it for later.
  • Long-press an item to edit it, attach photos, or move it to another list.
  • Send a list as a text message, or to another Lists user with its photos.
  • Share a list live with family through your own Google Drive, so everyone sees changes as they happen. This is optional: Lists only connects to Google if you turn it on.
  • Every delete asks first and can be undone.

Install

  1. On your Android phone (Android 10 or newer), open the latest release.
  2. Under Assets, tap Lists-x.y.z.apk to download it.
  3. Open the downloaded file. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from your browser. Allow it, then tap Install.

Updates

The app checks for new versions once a day, or from ⋮ → Check for updates. When one is available, tap Update. Your lists and photos are kept.

Privacy

Your lists stay on your phone, or in your own Google Drive if you share them live. Nothing is sent to the developer. Read the privacy policy.
